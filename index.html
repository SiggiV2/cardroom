The games

Seven classics around one quiet table.

Solitaire for a peaceful moment, card games to share with friends, and puzzles for a rainy day.

  • Spider Solitaire

    Sort the cards from King to Ace, with one, two or four suits.

  • TriPeaks Solitaire

    Clear the three peaks, one card higher or lower.

  • Castle Solitaire

    A calm, thoughtful solitaire with every card face up.

  • Gin Rummy

    Sets and runs for two: you and the computer, or a friend.

  • Rummy

    Lay your melds on the table, for 2 to 4 players.

  • Hearts

    Dodge the hearts and the Queen of Spades, with three computer players.

Easy to play

Easy on the eyes, and easy on the hands.

Every screen is large, clear and calm, with nothing hidden in tiny menus.

  • Big, clear cards

    Large corners show every card at a glance, even when cards overlap. An optional four-colour deck gives every suit its own colour.

  • Tap or drag

    Tap a card to play it, or drag it there if you prefer.

  • Hint and Undo

    Every game has a Hint button for when you are stuck, and the three solitaires let you undo any move.

  • How to play

    Each card game has a short guide under Help, with little animations that show every step.

  • Stop any time

    Unfinished games are kept, so you can carry on later from the Home screen.

Play with friends

In Gin Rummy and Rummy, choose an easy, medium or hard computer, or take turns with family at the kitchen table. Cards stay hidden between turns, so nobody peeks. Hearts is played against three computer players.

  • Easy, Medium or Hard computer in Gin Rummy and Rummy
  • Gin Rummy for two, Rummy for up to four, on one iPad
  • Clear scores after every hand
